Privacy Policy — Ayah Scroll

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Summary. Ayah Scroll has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your bookmarks, notes, reading history, plan and settings stay on your device.

What stays on your device

Bookmarks, notes, reading history and streaks, reading plan, settings, recent searches and the place you stopped. You can export them to a file, import a file, or delete everything in Settings → Privacy.

What the app downloads

The app contacts the internet only to fetch content, and sends no personal data with those requests (the request itself reveals your IP address to the server, as for any website):

  • api.quran.com (Quran Foundation): word-by-word meanings and tafsir for an ayah you open.
  • everyayah.com: the recitation audio for the ayah you play.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device after you grant permission. No notification content or token is sent to us or to any server.

Permissions

Notifications (reminders), network access, and background audio playback. Two optional permissions are asked for only when you use the feature:

  • Location (Qibla compass and prayer times): approximate location only. Your coordinates stay on the device and Ayah Scroll never sends them anywhere. To find your city, the coordinates are passed to your phone's built-in geocoder from Apple or Google, and only the city and country name are sent to AlAdhan (api.aladhan.com) to look up that day's prayer times. If you don't allow location, you can type a city instead.
  • Microphone and speech recognition (Recitation check): while you check a recitation, your voice is passed to your phone's built-in speech recognition from Apple or Google, which processes it on the device or on that company's speech service depending on the phone. Ayah Scroll itself does not store or upload it. The optional Pro engine, when offered, sends the recording to our recitation server, which checks it and deletes it.

The app does not use the camera, contacts or storage permissions.

Crash reports

The app does not send crash reports.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
